Three things make a promise stick.

  1. 1

    Write it where people can see it

    A goal with a number and a date. Holdfast turns it into a sheet your partners can open any time.

  2. 2

    Show up and show the receipt

    Log the session in under fifteen seconds. Add a photo, a watch screenshot, or a gym check-in when the challenge asks for one.

  3. 3

    Let the streak do the arguing

    Consistency is tallied week by week. Miss one and your partners see the gap — that's the whole mechanism.
